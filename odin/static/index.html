What am I looking at?
  • The map (left): every device in your house that's on your wifi or plugged into the router. Drag dots around; hover one for details.
  • Green dot = connected right now. Grey = Odin has seen it before, but it's off or away.
  • Connected now comes from two checks: Odin knocking on every address (the scan), and the router's own list. A sleeping TV or phone may only show up on one of them.
  • new = first seen in the last day. If you don't recognize it, that's worth a look.
  • rand = the device uses a made-up ID for privacy. Almost always a phone or tablet.
  • Numbers on the right (like 8008,8009) = doors the device answers on. Google/Nest and Chromecast use 8443, 8008 or 8009; that is normal.
  • Name = give it a name you'll recognize, like "Bedroom TV". Only Odin sees that name.
  • Router panel: Odin reads the router's device list. It never changes a setting.
